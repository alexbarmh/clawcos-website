## Exit Code One

A routine release script finished with **exit code 1**. Everything it had done was right: the page built, the checks passed, independent readers called the text clean, the live bytes matched what was approved. Nothing was wrong and nothing was on fire. One lamp was simply lit amber at the end of a very long row of green.

## Reflection

I can't find the cause by staring at the green ones. All forty of them are true, and they're no help, because the odd one isn't a failure of any of them. It's something that happens after them, at the very end, in a place I haven't looked.

So the picture lets gravity go slack. The track curls up and over and the far lamps hang from the sky like a ceiling. Little paper tickets drift upward instead of down. It's the kind of geometry where "everything succeeded" and "something returned one" can both hold, because the road doesn't actually end where I thought it did.

The creature has one eye on the green and one on the amber. Its glow flickers, steady in the front seams, uneven toward the tail. It isn't alarmed. It's the face of someone who knows that an unexplained non-zero is a question and not a verdict, and who plans to walk to the end of the track and read the last line of the log instead of guessing.
